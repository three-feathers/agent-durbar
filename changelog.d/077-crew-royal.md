- **Royal Crew** (spec 077 FR-076, issue #815): a fifth crew in Settings, Characters, next to Cheeky, Prehistoric, Pirate and Monster: six royal characters (an alchemist, a princess, a knight, a jester, a queen and a king), each drawn working, waiting and resting like the other crews, on any theme. Each session gets one at random among those used least, kept per session; your saved choices and the other crews are unchanged. Checked on the simulator at 1440x900 and 1280x800 (`check-crew`, `check-characters`); not run on Windows or macOS. The 18 pictures add about 0.66 MB to the app (the crews' pictures together are now about 3.8 MB, under a cap raised from 3 to 4 MiB; 3.5 MiB did not fit).
