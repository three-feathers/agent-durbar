- **Search conversations** (spec 095, issue 877): a box in the top bar (or its magnifier on a narrow window; **Ctrl+Shift+F**, ⇧⌘F on a Mac, changeable in Settings > Keyboard, also in Ctrl+K) finds a word or phrase in the title, your messages and the agent's replies of every open session and every earlier session of the folders you have open (earlier Copilot and Codex sessions by title only; tool output and hidden sessions are not searched). Results show in the List view, grouped *In the office* and *Earlier sessions*, with up to three masked excerpts and the words marked; Enter or a click opens the chat (or resumes the session, or opens the VS Code chat) at that message. Local only: nothing is stored, the search runs in Durbar on your computer, excerpts are masked before you see them, and the end line says what was not searched. Simulator-checked at 1440x900 and 1280x800 (`shots-095`); not tried on a real store.
