- **Hovering a floor says which floor it is** (spec 092 FR-085, issue #1958): with two or more floors on, the empty part of a floor screen shows "<floor name>, floor 2 of 3" next to the pointer (in All floors, the floor under the pointer), and a lift button's tooltip starts with the same words, adds "(you are here)" on the current floor, and keeps the attention words on the next line. Parts with their own tip keep it; the Real office draws its own storeys and shows no hint. Checked in the simulator at 1440x900 and 1280x800 (`shots-092-lift`); not with a touch screen or a screen reader.
