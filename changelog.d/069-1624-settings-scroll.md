- **Settings keeps its place when you switch an MCP server off or on** (spec 069 FR-069, issue 1624): the switches of the Copilot CLI list and the Claude Code list rebuilt the whole Settings page, which jumped back to the top and dropped the focus. A repaint of Settings now keeps the scroll position and the focused control, also while agent app accounts, quota and policy rows arrive after it; only your own scrolling, a key or a click moves the page. New check `check-069-toggle-scroll` reads twelve switches at both window sizes. Not verified on macOS or Windows.
