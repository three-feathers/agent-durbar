- **Auto-follow work, opt in** (spec 052 FR-182..FR-190, issue 686): a new switch in Settings (off by default, kept on this computer; main answers a real `ok`) makes a sent message take you back to the office with that agent's desk marked and focused, and open its chat (focus on the chat title, never on Allow or Deny) when it finishes, asks, errors or reaches its limit. It never answers or approves anything, waits while you type, have a dialog or menu open, or chose another agent, and respects reduced motion (no animation). A strip under the top bar shows Previous and Next over the attention queue (the Needs you inbox order, then unread replies) with the position and count (keys `[` and `]`), a Pause button, and, when nothing needs you, Next returns to the office with the last desk focused. Checked in the simulator (`check:ui` check-auto-follow, both sizes, 15 agents) and `test:autofollow`; not run against a real agent session.
