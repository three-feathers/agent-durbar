- **Real office mode, phase 1** (spec 097): Settings > Office layout has a third choice, **Real office**: the same agents in a realistic open-plan office, a person at each desk in a pose for its status, a monitor and a name plate with the status word, one zone per folder, and the places along the back wall with their visitors standing on the floor in front of them at a real size against the door (name tags above their heads) (one fixture per place, wider when crowded); whoever is at the Terminal jogs in place beside it (new running pictures for all 15 people, no treadmill). Status lights: warm lamp while working, amber call light when it needs you, small steady red on an error, orange at the limit, off when asleep, and a slightly darker room when few agents are active; every light repeats a word or icon, none flashes, they stand still with reduced motion or Serious mode and are not drawn with forced colours. Flat stays the default and loads none of it. Adds 6.1 MB of pictures (15 invented people, MIT). Checked on the simulator (layout check at 1440x900 and 1280x800, unit tests for the layout and the lights, an asset test); live screen text, motion, crews and manual lighting come later. While Real office is shown, the whole app takes its look: a dark wood-and-glass top bar and toasts, frosted-glass menus, popovers and dialogs, warm neutrals and oak-and-amber buttons and focus rings, every text pair checked at WCAG AA; status colours and fonts stay, forced colours use the system colours, and leaving Real office brings your theme back. With the Cheeky or Prehistoric characters, Settings says Real office shows the Office people until that crew's world is ready.
