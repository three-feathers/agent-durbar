- **Finished helpers no longer fill the memory** (spec 005 FR-240, FR-241, issue #1418): an agent kept every helper it ever started, with its whole activity in the chat, its card and its event log, for as long as the agent stayed in the office. An agent now keeps its newest 50 finished and 20 failed helpers in full; older ones leave the office and the helper lists, and their block in the chat becomes one line with the helper's name, result and status. One line at the top of the chat says how many earlier helpers are not kept in this view. A helper that is still running, the one whose activity you have open, and one you are focused on or selecting text in are never removed. Checked with `test:subretire` and the `check-helper-retention` simulator driver (Open office, Real office and Retro arcade, 1440x900 and 1280x800); Linux only.
