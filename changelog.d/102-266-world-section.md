- **Settings > World: pick where you work from picture cards** (spec 102 FR-300, FR-301, FR-302; T265 and T266 in part): the Display section's *Office layout* and *World* rows are replaced by a **World** section with a card for **Plain** (today's flat office) and one for the **Photo office**, each with a picture of the app in that world, its name, one line and *Built in*. Nothing is migrated and your choice is kept where it was. Worlds that are not open yet are not listed. The pictures are taken from the simulator (`simscene=preview`, `scripts/shots-102-world-previews.js`), no image generation. Checked by `check-settings-worlds` (with mutants) and `shots-102-world-previews`.
