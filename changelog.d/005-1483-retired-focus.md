- **A helper's head that has the keyboard focus is not retired** (spec 005 FR-242, issue #1483): in the Real office, with the `+K` strip open, a finished helper whose head held the focus could be retired by the next end and the focus fell to the page. It is now kept while the head has the focus (as the block in the chat already was) and retired by the next end once the focus leaves. Checked by `check-helper-retention` with `CHR_WORLD=real` (mutant: without the rule it fails).
