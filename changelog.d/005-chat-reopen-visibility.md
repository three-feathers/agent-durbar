- **Long chats open faster** (spec 005 FR-210, FR-211): when you leave a chat, its older messages are marked with their measured height, so reopening a chat of 4,000 messages takes about 0.25 s instead of 2.4 s in the simulator and the messages far from the view are not laid out. Scrolling (including big jumps), the end of the chat while a turn streams, copying across messages and keyboard focus are unchanged. A long history opened for the first time is not faster. Simulator only; not run on Windows or macOS.
