- **The keyboard focus ring on the Tools, Settings, Office and List buttons and on the ⋯ menu items reads on the retro look** (spec 052, issue #1615): the ring was yellow on the cream strip and the cream menu (1.0 to 1.2:1; the pressed Office button was yellow on yellow). The menu items now use the page's ring colour and the four buttons an inset ring in the text colour of their own fill (15.3:1 in Retro Pixel; 3.8 to 12.1:1 in a user theme with the retro look). The buttons on the dark bar keep the yellow ring. The arcade bar is unchanged and stays a known gap.
