- **Recent order in the chat's agent rail** (spec 113): a *Folders | Recent* switch under Your door. *Recent* lists every session in one flat list: whoever needs you first, then the sessions you last wrote to, newest first, each with its folder under the name and how long ago you wrote to it; sessions you have not written to since Durbar started wait in a folded *Not used since Durbar started* group, and the open chat's row is always shown. Only a message you typed moves a row. The choice, the times you wrote (ids and times, never your text) and the group's fold are remembered like the collapsed folders; *Sort* applies to *Folders* only. The rail's list scrolls 28 px more at 1280x800. Checked on the simulator at 1440x900 and 1280x800 and at 180 px (macOS only); the filter field is not part of this change, and screen readers (VoiceOver, NVDA) have not been tried.
