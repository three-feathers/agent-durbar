- **Network share paths shown whole** (spec 012, issue #2060): on your own screen, a Windows network share path is shown whole when its parts look like plain names: server, share and folders, in error texts, helper cards, the activity line, toasts, strips and what the screen reader says. A path with a long unbroken run of letters or digits in any part keeps the earlier hiding. Text that leaves the screen keeps the earlier masking: *Copy error text* and *Copy diagnostics* still hide a share path's server, share and next folder. Checked with labelled tests, a differential against the earlier masking and mutants; not checked on Windows or macOS with a real share.
