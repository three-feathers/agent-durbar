Settings > Brain view turns it off (the chat gets its full width back) and Settings > Brain motion picks Full, Calm or Still; your system's reduced motion, Still highlights, Motion off and a low battery make it Still. In a window under 1100 px wide the rail starts collapsed; the chat header's Brain button opens it. The preview flag is gone (`DURBAR_BRAIN=0|1` only overrides a development run). Checked in the simulator in Flat, the Photo office and the Retro arcade at 1440x900 and 1280x800; not on macOS or Windows, not with a screen reader.
