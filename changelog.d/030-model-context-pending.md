- **A real Copilot session could not switch model, effort or window at all** (spec 030 FR-067): the switch asked the runtime to refuse any model it counted as unavailable, and on a paid plan it refused every model but Auto. Durbar no longer asks that, and checks the request against the account's own model list first. Not checked on a free plan or on a model the plan lacks: such a model may now fail at the next message, with the runtime's error, and the chip keeps the chosen one until you switch back.
- **Model, effort and context chosen one after another stay together** (spec 030 FR-060..FR-066, owner report): during a reply, on a session that has not started and after a slow answer, a second choice replaced the first, depending on the order. Each choice now builds on what the session runs next, a queued switch holds all of them and Cancel leaves nothing; a new model keeps the effort and the long window when it offers them and says so when it cannot. Claude Code and Codex sessions are checked against their own model list (their own models were refused), and Claude Code's default now offers its efforts. Checked with `test-model-choice`, the adapter tests and the driver `check-model-choice` at both sizes.
