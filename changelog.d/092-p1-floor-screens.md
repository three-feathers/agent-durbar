- **Floors as places, step 1: one floor at a time** (spec 092 phase 1, issue 782): with two or more floors on, the office shows one floor's rooms and the shared lobby, each at the best density for its own agents (the 15-agent rule holds on each floor at 1440x900 and 1280x800). **PageDown** goes to the next floor that is on and **PageUp** to the previous one (Fn+Down / Fn+Up on a Mac laptop), instantly, once per press, announced as "Floor Client B, 2 of 3"; a floor that is taller than the window scrolls first and changes floor only from its edge; the keys keep their own meaning in text boxes, selects, menus and dialogs. The current floor is remembered; if it is switched off or deleted the view moves to the nearest floor that is on. A floor with nothing on it says so and keeps the lobby; a lobby visitor from another floor names its floor and does not walk. The status pills, the inbox, the title and the usage total still count the whole building. With one floor on nothing changes. Not yet: the lift panel with attention badges, the slide and "open an agent, land on its floor" (later steps of the spec). Checked on the simulator and with unit tests (`test:floornav`, `shots-092`, `check:office` per floor screen); not tried on a real Mac or Windows keyboard.
