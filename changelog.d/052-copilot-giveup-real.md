- **A message after a stuck Stop is no longer lost, and a session reopened after one keeps working** (spec 052 FR-228..FR-230, issue #1028; checked on a real Copilot runtime on Linux, not on macOS or Windows): when Copilot has not answered Stop after 5 seconds the row says "Stop sent: Copilot has not answered yet" instead of only "Aborted". A message sent then waits for the Stop to land (at most 30 seconds, then the chat says it was not sent); before, the late Stop cleared it with no error. Reopening a session whose End has not landed waits the same way; before, it opened, and the next call failed with "Session not found".
- **The spending limit after a stuck Stop, and a raise that was too small** (spec 052 FR-231..FR-235, issue #1028; measured on a real Copilot runtime on Linux, not on macOS or Windows): a message sent after a stuck Stop at the limit waits on the limit card; before, the card could read "stopped here" while the message stayed behind. Stop at the limit no longer shows "No response was returned". A raise that stays under what was used keeps the card and the message; before, the message was lost, or the card said "credits added" and the turn ended. A session over its limit opens again and asks at the next message; before, Copilot refused to reopen it.
