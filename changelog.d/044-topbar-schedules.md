- **A Schedules button in the top bar** (spec 044 FR-027..FR-035, issue #523; owner's idea: scheduled prompts were hard to find). A clock with the number of active schedules, left of **Needs you** (the word shows from 1920px, 2040px on a Mac, and in Simple); it opens **Settings > Schedules** at its heading, from the office, the list, a chat (**Ctrl+Shift+S**, ⇧⌘S on a Mac) and **Ctrl+K**. A small **!** on the clock shows a run that was missed or failed since you last looked (done one-time schedules included; busy and paused skips never), and a pause mark shows **Pause all**. Hidden in the demo and in an empty office.
