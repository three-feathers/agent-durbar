- **A chat keeps its place across the Browser tab** (spec 005 FR-235, issue #1168): a chat that was following the end is at the end again, and still following, when you come back from Browser to Chat (also when the agent wrote meanwhile); a chat you had scrolled up keeps its position. The cause was the collapsed chat losing its side padding, so its messages re-wrapped wider and grew back unnoticed; it now keeps its width, also where scrollbars take room (Windows, Linux; the chat reserves the scrollbar's space). Checked on macOS only, with a simulated classic scrollbar, by the simulator driver `check-browser-follow` at 1440x900 and 1280x800.
