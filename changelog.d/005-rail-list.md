- **The agent rail** (spec 005 FR-190, FR-194, FR-216; spec 052 FR-215, FR-216): with a chat open, a rail on the left replaces the strip at the top. It lists every agent by folder, with the folder's count; whoever needs you comes first, in a taller row that says how long they have waited and what they ask, and their folder moves up. **Your door** at its top shows the Needs you count and opens the queue. A folder collapses and expands with a click, Enter, Space or a double click, still showing its count and how many in it need you; opening a chat in it expands it, and the collapsed folders are remembered per window. On a Mac the rail's top row and the chat header move the window. Checked in the simulator by `check-rail` at 1440x900 and 1280x800 (all 15 agents, no page overflow, two row heights, contrast) and `test-railorder`; not tried on a real Mac or Windows.
  Highlight: **The agent rail**: every agent beside the chat, by folder, and who needs you first.
