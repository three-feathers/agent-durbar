- **Fewer harmless command words hidden, and the chat header masks names** (spec 012 FR-196 and FR-197, issues #1981 and #1999): the task line, a tool chip and what Copy copies no longer hide the word after a switch flag such as `--show-signature`, a plain switch value such as `PasswordAuthentication=no`, or a short query word such as `?state=open`; the chat's header name, its tooltip and the chat's and composer's screen-reader labels now hide a secret-shaped value or a home folder in a session's name. Checked by unit tests with invented values and a comparison against the previous masks; not checked with a screen reader or on macOS and Windows.
