- **Fewer harmless command words hidden, and the chat header masks names** (spec 012 FR-196 and FR-197, issues #1981, #1999 and #2060): the task line, a tool chip and what Copy copies no longer hide the word after a display or negated flag such as `--show-signature` or `--no-session-persistence`, or a plain switch value such as `PasswordAuthentication=no`; a filter word in a link such as `?state=open` stays readable (also when a note saves the link), while a sign-in link keeps its code and state hidden; the chat's header name, its tooltip and the chat's and composer's screen-reader labels now hide a secret-shaped value or a home folder in a session's name. Checked by unit tests with invented values and a comparison against the previous masks; not checked with a screen reader or on macOS and Windows.
