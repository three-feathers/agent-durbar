- **Readable file chips in the Retro arcade** (spec 070 FR-081, issue #1278): in the dark Retro arcade world, attachment chips, ask tags and selected rows used a light-grey fill with blue text at about 2.2:1 (hard to read at 12 px). They now use a dark blue fill with light blue text (7.5:1). Other looks are unchanged; the contrast check now covers these pairs in every look. The same fix covers the other tone-coloured badges and notes in the arcade (focus-mode toggle, finished sub-agent tag, warning and error tones, quick replies): their text on dark fills was 1.2 to 2.7:1 and is now at least 5.9:1. Badges and notes coloured by the status pair (not a tone) are not part of this fix.
