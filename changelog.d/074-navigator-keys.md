- **Go to keys, after a test drive** (spec 074, issue #587): Go to opens with **Ctrl+F** (⌘F on a Mac) instead of Ctrl+J ("F for floor"; a shortcut you set yourself is kept). Checked with unit tests and the simulator driver (mutation-checked); not tried on a real Mac or Windows machine.
