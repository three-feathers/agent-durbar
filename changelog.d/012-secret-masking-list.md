- **The secret mask, compared with public lists** (spec 012 FR-080, issue 1009): debug logs, desk lines, notes, MCP output and tool labels now hide about 70 more token shapes (npm, Google, Stripe, AWS, Azure, GitLab, Hugging Face and others), private key blocks, `password=...` style values, cookies and signed URLs; there is now one mask instead of two levels. Every entry of gitleaks, GitHub's list and detect-secrets is in a [coverage table](specs/012-claude-codex-adapters/secret-coverage.md) with why it is or is not hidden. No list is complete: secrets with no fixed shape, and encoded or split ones, still pass. A long crafted line can no longer stall the app for 4.5 s while it is masked.
