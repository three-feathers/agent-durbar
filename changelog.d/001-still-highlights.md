- **Still highlights (no motion)** (spec 001 FR-192, issue #780, WCAG 2.2.2): Settings > Highlights has a switch that keeps the coloured glow and the New badge on desks that need you or have a new reply, but stops the movement that goes with them: the person's jumping and pulsing, the glow's breathing, the badge's pulse and the waiting badge's pings, the waiting person's waving hand and the tap on the desk after a minute. In the Real office the whole room stands still. It does not stop every animation (the system's reduced motion setting does that; some small ones, such as a theme's own figures, still follow it). Off by default and saved on this computer. A failed save says so and the switch shows what is really saved. `durbar_settings` (MCP) now reports `stillHighlights` next to `seriousMode`. Checked in `check-unread` (simulator, both sizes), `test:stillhighlights` (main's handler on a stand-in `electron` with a real store) and `test:ipcchannels`; not run on a packaged app, Windows or macOS.
