- **A long chat no longer fills the memory** (spec 005 FR-238, FR-239, issue #1380): every agent's chat kept every message and tool call for as long as the app stayed open, for closed chats too, so the window grew by about 35 MB an hour with the simulator (Ruben saw about 2 GB a day). A chat now keeps its newest 300 messages, the newest 300 steps of a turn or helper still running, and at most 2,000 steps in all, never your last question or the last reply (a single huge turn loses its oldest steps instead), also while the window is hidden; questions and permission cards waiting for you and the live parts of the turn are always kept, and one line at the top says how many earlier messages and steps are not kept in this view (the agent app's session keeps its own record). Scrolled up in an open chat, what you read stays where it is, and a note saved from a message stays on that message. Checked with `test:chattrim` and the `check-chat-memory` simulator driver (nodes, listeners and heap flat after the warm-up; Open office, Real office and Retro arcade); Linux only.
