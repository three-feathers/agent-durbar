- **`check:themes` no longer hangs on macOS** (spec 008 FR-125/FR-126, #670): available memory there counts free, inactive and speculative pages from `vm_stat` (not free pages only), and a parallel worker that has waited 120 s for memory starts its job only when no other job is running, so none waits forever and the load stays at one job under low memory. Ran on Paco's Mac (14/14 shards, vm_stat 2848 MB vs freemem 186 MB); the 120 s fallback was not exercised there (unit tests only).
