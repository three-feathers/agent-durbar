- **Characters cards: the arrow keys only move the focus** (spec 102 FR-301, FR-303, issue 1724): in Settings, Characters, the arrow keys, Home and End now move the focus from card to card (skipping the closed Retro pixel) without choosing; Space or Enter chooses, as on the World cards. Before, each arrow press also changed the Characters choice. The simulator driver `check-characters` covers both sizes.
