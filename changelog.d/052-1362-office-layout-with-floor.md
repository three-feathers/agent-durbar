- **The office no longer overflows its floor for a frame when the floor gets shorter** (spec 052 FR-243, issue #1362): turning Auto-follow on makes the floor 31 px shorter, and the office was drawn for the new height only a frame later (a quarter of a second under load), so desks hung over the floor's edge and a click in that moment could land on another agent's desk. The office is now drawn in the same step as the floor changes size. Checked in the simulator on a Mac, Open office and Real office, 1440x900 and 1280x800.
