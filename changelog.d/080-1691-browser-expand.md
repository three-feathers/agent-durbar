- **Expand the browser in a chat** (spec 080 FR-134..FR-139, issue #1691): the browser bar's new last button, **Expand browser**, makes the Browser tab fill the whole window, so a page is as big as in your usual browser; **Back to small browser** (the same button) or **Esc** puts it back next to the chat, with your draft still there. Esc pressed inside the web page still goes to the page. Checked on the simulator in Flat, the Photo office and the Retro arcade at 1440x900 and 1280x800.
