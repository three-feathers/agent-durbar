- **A finished agent's reply is marked "New" until you have seen it** (specs 001 and 005, issue #691): when an agent finishes while its chat is not shown, its desk, list row and chat-strip item get a **New** badge (dot and word, an outline on the desk, "new reply not read yet" in its accessible name, one announcement, a single pulse only when motion is allowed). It is a marker, not a state: the counts, sort, filters and permissions do not change, and an unread agent stays on its desk instead of resting on the couch. It clears when its chat is shown or with **Mark as read** in the card menu (also Shift+F10 / the Menu key on a focused desk or list row, new); closing the chat or going back to the office does not. Not kept across a restart. Simulator-checked (`npm run check:unread`, 67 unit checks, 1440x900 and 1280x800); not run on real sessions.
