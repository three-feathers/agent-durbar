- **Text popups stay open while you select their text** (spec 052 FR-237): a drag that starts in a dialog and ends outside it (selecting a field's text and overshooting) no longer closes it. It did for 11 popups: the Rename, Rename floor, Place, palette, Schedules, spending limit, New session and New profile dialogs, the floors popover's new floor name, the Needs you inbox and the keyboard sheet. A click into a folder's rename field no longer closes the Folders panel. One shared helper decides; checked in the simulator with real mouse and keyboard input at both window sizes, and by unit tests.
