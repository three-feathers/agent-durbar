- **Coffee break when a usage limit is reached** (spec 068, owner's idea). An agent whose plan window, quota or rate limit is used up (Claude Code, Codex, Copilot), or that reached your spending limit, waits in a new **coffee corner** in the lobby instead of showing an error, with the reset time when the agent app gives one. It comes back by itself when the limit resets and that costs nothing extra (one fixed message to continue), asks first for a quota that may be billed, and never goes past your spending limit or resumes after a restart. Settings > *Coffee break* switches to always asking. Themes name the corner (Chai stall, The Tea Room, Snack bar). Unit tests and a simulator driver (`scripts/shots-068-coffee.js`, `simbreak=N`); not run against a real limit.
