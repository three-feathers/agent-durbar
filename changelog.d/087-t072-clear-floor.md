- **Allow all now turns Copilot's OS sandbox off in the running session** (spec 087 FR-022c, T066, T072, issue #806): switching a Copilot session from Read-only or Ask first to Allow all lowers its sandbox in place, and the Allow all confirmation says "Copilot’s OS sandbox is also turned off for this session."; switching back re-sends it. A real probe on Linux (Copilot CLI 1.0.98, 3 prompts) showed the sandbox clears in place and comes back; Durbar's own switch is tested on the simulator and with fakes, and macOS and Windows are not verified. If Copilot refuses or does not answer, the sandbox is treated as still on and *Restart without the sandbox* stays offered. The T066 probe's cleanup now also removes the empty `.git/hooks` and `.git/config` placeholders the sandbox leaves in its session folder.
