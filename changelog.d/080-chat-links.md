- **Chat links open in the session browser** (spec 080 T001, T013, T017, T018, issue #556): a click on a link in a chat opens it in that session's Browser tab (asking the one-time note first; *Not now* sends it to your usual browser that time). Ctrl+click (⌘+click on a Mac) and the link's right-click menu (*Open in system browser*) use your usual browser, and *Settings > Open chat links in* switches the plain click. A mail link only offers *Open* and waits for your click; any other kind of link is refused. Up to four pages stay loaded: the least recently shown others are dropped and reload when shown (*Reloaded to save memory*); a page you typed into or submitted a form from is never dropped. Checked on the simulator (its own test pages) on Linux; memory use was not measured.
