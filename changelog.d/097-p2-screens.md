- **Real office, phase 2** (spec 097): the monitors show each agent's newest output, masked like the desk's live readout and repainted at most every 2 seconds; hover a monitor or tab to its desk to zoom it, Esc closes; with 4 agents or fewer the monitors are big enough to read. People move in small steps (typing, waving, thinking, resting; visitors sip, knock and jog), and nothing moves with reduced motion, in Serious mode, in a hidden window or out of view.
- **Masking** (spec 097): a token with an invisible character inside (zero-width, soft hyphen, a joiner) is now masked on desks, titles and Real office monitors.
- **Real office: people move smoothly** (spec 097): each person breathes and sways on their own timing, a typing person's head and hands move on their own, and someone going to a place zooms out at the desk and in at the place, leaving a post-it on the desk that says where. The room stays still when someone leaves; desks are a little smaller at 15 agents as a result. Reduced motion and Serious mode stop it as before; hidden or off-screen desks pause.
