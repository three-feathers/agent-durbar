- **Monster Crew** (spec 077 FR-075, issue #815): a fourth crew in Settings, Characters, next to Cheeky, Prehistoric and Pirate: six friendly monsters (Frankenstein, a ghost, a mummy, a sea creature, a vampire and a werewolf), each drawn working, waiting and resting like the other crews, on any theme. Each session gets one at random among those used least, kept per session; your saved choices and the other crews are unchanged. Checked on the simulator at 1440x900 and 1280x800 (`check-crew`, `check-characters`); not run on Windows or macOS.
