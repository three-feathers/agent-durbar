Poll host sheet · EA Summit talk

Thank you for running the polls. Seven polls are spread through the talk and a word cloud comes near the end. When a poll slide comes up, it shows the question with a "POLL N — LIVE IN TEAMS NOW" banner: launch that poll. Polls 1 to 5 ask what people do today, honestly; there is no right answer while they vote. After the vote the presenter shows "the 2026 way" on the next press.

How, in Teams: in the meeting, open Polls (Forms) → New poll → Multiple choice, paste the question, then one option per field. Keep results visible. For the end, choose Word cloud (or an open text poll if word cloud is not offered). Tip: prepare all eight as drafts before the talk in this order, then just press Launch on each cue.
