Cheeky Crew — Second Character Pack

Objective

Generate 18 separate PNG images representing 6 original cartoon characters, each with 3 distinct poses.

Generate ONE image per request. Never combine multiple characters or poses into a sheet, grid, collage, or multi-panel composition.

Global Visual Style

All 18 illustrations must share exactly the same visual style:

* Modern cartoon character portraits designed as app avatars.
* Bold, clean black outlines.
* Warm cel-shaded comic/sticker aesthetic.
* Friendly young adults with expressive, slightly exaggerated eyes.
* Fun, unconventional, slightly rebellious personalities.
* Polished professional digital illustration.
* Head-and-shoulders bust, centred, cropped at mid-chest.
* Square 1254 × 1254 PNG.
* TRUE transparent alpha background.
* No checkerboard, floor, shadow, vignette, frame, or border.
* No text, letters, numbers, logos, brands, or symbols on clothing or accessories.
* No rude gestures.
* Office-safe, playful and a little cheeky.
* Original characters, not based on celebrities or real people.

CRITICAL CONSISTENCY RULE: Preserve the exact same face, hairstyle, skin colour, clothing, accessories, proportions, and overall personality across all three poses of each character.

Three Poses

WORKING

* Looking toward the right side, as though working at an off-screen computer.
* Focused, confident half-smile.
* Both hands outside the image.
* No resting prop.

WAITING

* Facing the viewer directly.
* Warm and friendly smile.
* One raised OPEN hand waving.
* Palm facing the viewer, with five natural, clearly visible fingers.
* No resting prop.

RESTING

* Eyes gently closed.
* Relaxed, content smile.
* Holding the character-specific prop with BOTH hands in front of the chest.
* Entire prop and both hands visible.

Generation Procedure

For each character:

1. Generate the WORKING portrait first.
2. Use the WORKING portrait as the identity and style reference when generating WAITING.
3. Use the preceding images of the same character as references for RESTING.
4. Preserve facial structure, hairstyle, accessories, skin tone, and outfit exactly.
5. Change only the pose, facial expression, visibility of hands, and specified prop.
6. Never use a different character's portrait as an identity reference.
7. Maintain the same global illustration style throughout all 18 images.

* Consistent identity across all three poses.
* Correct hair, glasses, clothing, and accessories.
* Clearly visible open palm with five fingers in WAITING.
* Closed eyes and both hands holding the specified prop in RESTING.
* No text, numbers, logos, or unwanted symbols.
* Proper head-and-shoulders composition.
* Complete, non-corrupted PNG.
* 1254 × 1254 resolution.
* Real transparent alpha background.

Final deliverable: 18 individually named, independent transparent PNG images suitable for a modern app avatar system.
