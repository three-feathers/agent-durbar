# Cheeky Crew real art: provenance (spec 077 FR-054)

These 39 pictures (13 characters x working, waiting, resting) are the real art of the Cheeky Crew (issue #558). They are wired in: `src/renderer/crew.js` lists the 13 characters, `scripts/copy-assets.js` ships only the `.webp` files of this folder (not this file or the prompt files), and `npm run test:crew-assets` checks every picture against these rows. The flat placeholders and `scripts/crew-placeholders.py` are gone.

Format: 352x352 WebP (q82; `surfer-waiting.webp` and `surfer-resting.webp` re-encoded at q80 on 2026-10-23 because q82 was over the 40 KB cap, FR-050), resized from Paco's 1254x1254 RGBA PNGs (the originals are not committed), no EXIF/XMP/ICC chunks. The old Einstein-like scientist from batch 1 is dropped (owner, 2026-10-23); the scientist here is the new one from batch 2.

Batch 2 notes (2026-10-23):
- **Names.** The prompt named the files `nerd`, `metal` and `nerd-girl`; we keep `bookworm`, `metalhead` and `bob-glasses`. Each row gives the prompt's name.
- **What the prompt asked to avoid.** The rocker: "Rockabilly inspiration without resembling Elvis Presley or any other celebrity". The scientist: "MUST NOT RESEMBLE EINSTEIN", with a list of features to avoid.
- **Where the result differs from the prompt.** The metalhead's vest came out dark grey or black denim, not blue.

Replaced art (owner decision, Paco, 2026-10-26, spec 077 FR-059 delta, T070): 30 of the 39 pictures are new, made by Paco in ChatGPT (image generation) and supplied on 2026-10-26: all three poses of sporty, disco, skater, hacker, buzzcut, bookworm, metalhead, scientist, gamer and surfer (the surfer is redrawn with a bigger head and vivid colours). punk, rocker and bob-glasses are unchanged. Same conversion as before (352x352, q82, alpha, no metadata); `gamer-working`, `gamer-waiting`, `gamer-resting` and `surfer-waiting` were encoded at q80 to stay under the 40 KB cap (FR-050). The prompts of the nine characters other than the surfer were not supplied with this batch (owner to provide); none is invented here. The rows below say what each file is now; the 2026-10-23 sign-off in the next paragraph covered the earlier art; the new art has its own sign-off: Paco looked at a contact sheet of all 13 characters (the 30 new pictures and the 9 unchanged ones) on 2026-10-26 and answered "esta bien así" (it is fine as it is) in his Mac agent's terminal, none to redo.
