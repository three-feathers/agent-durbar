Cheeky crew — prompts para ChatGPT (Fase 1: 4 personajes × 3 poses = 12 imágenes)

CÓMO USARLOS
1. Abre un chat nuevo en ChatGPT y pega primero el bloque ESTILO (una sola vez).
2. Luego pide cada imagen con su línea de personaje + pose.
3. Descarga cada imagen en PNG y mándamela por Telegram con el nombre: punk-working, punk-waiting, punk-resting, etc.
4. Si alguna sale con texto, logos, símbolos o gestos de manos, pide "repite sin X". Yo hago el recorte, la compresión a WebP, la revisión y el registro en ASSETS.md.

ESTILO (pegar una vez al principio)
Make a series of character portraits for a desktop app, all in one consistent style:
- cute chibi cartoon, bold clean outlines, soft cel shading, warm friendly colours, like a sticker set
- BUST ONLY: head, shoulders and upper chest, arms down or hands just at the bottom edge; NO desk, NO computer, NO keyboard, NO chair, NO background objects
- the character faces slightly to the RIGHT (three-quarter view), as if looking at a screen on their right
- transparent background (PNG with alpha), square 1024x1024, character centred, small margin
- office-safe and friendly: NO text, NO letters or numbers, NO logos, NO brands, NO game or film characters, NO flags or symbols (no anarchy sign, no skulls on clothes), NO hand gestures (no peace sign, no horns, no pointing, no thumbs up), NO tongue out
- every image in the series must look like the same artist drew it

POSES (la misma para los 4 personajes)
- working: focused, eyes on the screen to the right, slight smile, shoulders relaxed as if typing
- waiting: looking at the viewer, eyebrows raised, patient friendly expression, one open hand raised at shoulder height (palm open, no gesture)
- resting: eyes closed, peaceful smile, head tilted a little, holding a plain steaming mug in both hands

PERSONAJES
1. punk: young person with a tall bright green mohawk, shaved sides, small silver ear rings, black leather jacket with silver studs on the shoulders, red t-shirt underneath; medium-light skin. Mug for resting: plain black mug.
2. scientist: older cheerful mad scientist, wild fluffy white hair, round brass lab goggles pushed up on the forehead, white lab coat with a pen in the pocket, bow tie; light skin with rosy cheeks. Mug for resting: plain white mug.
3. gamer: young person with long pink hair, pink over-ear headphones with small cat ears on top, lavender hoodie; tan skin. Mug for resting: plain pink mug.
4. hacker: young person in a dark grey hoodie with the hood up, purple hair showing under the hood, calm sly smile, eyes clearly visible (not hidden by shadow); deep brown skin. Mug for resting: plain dark mug.
