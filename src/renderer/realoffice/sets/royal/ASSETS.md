The asset rule (T238, FR-067) says "Paco approves each picture"; the owner Vikram Gorla took that role (offer: "I can approve Paco's pictures if you want", Telegram DM, 2026-11-08 11:27 UTC) and every frame below is approved by Vikram Gorla (owner) in Paco's place, Telegram DM, 2026-11-08 11:29 UTC, "They all look good". - **Made by:** Vikram's agent (Claude Code), 2026-11-08, on the owner's approval of the sample (Vikram, 2026-11-08 08:28 UTC) and of Codex image generation for character art (2026-10-31).
- **References given to every call:** the person's three approved crew portraits (`royal-<id>-working`, `-waiting`, `-resting` from `src/renderer/crew/art/`); sheet F2 also got that person's `knock` frame from sheet F1 as the body reference.
- **Cut and re-encoded** (Pillow, scratch scripts, not in the repo): sheets split at the emptiest column near each third; seated busts cut flat at the desk line (the lowest row still at least 85% as wide as the widest row of the bottom quarter), scaled to a median 343 px, set on one canvas per person with at least 10 px of transparent padding above the tallest pose; standing figures cropped to the figure and scaled to about 361 px; WebP quality 82 with alpha, no metadata; `set.json` holds numbers measured from the shipped pictures.
